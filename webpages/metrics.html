DAILY MAINTENANCE CALORIES

Your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is the total fuel needed to maintain your current physique. It combines your BMR (resting metabolism) with the Thermic Effect (calories burned during digestion) of your nutrition.

  • RECOMPOSITION: The "Sweet Spot" for building muscle and shedding fat simultaneously without the scale moving.
  • RECOVERY: Proper maintenance fuel prevents metabolic burnout and keeps your training intensity at peak levels.

CALORIE DEFICIT

To force your body to utilize stored adipose tissue (fat) for energy, you must create an energy gap. This tool calculates that gap with surgical precision.

  • THE 500 RULE: A 500kcal deficit is the "Gold Standard" for losing ~0.5kg of fat per week without losing muscle.
  • SUSTAINABILITY: Aggressive deficits (>750kcal) often lead to burnout and metabolic slowdown.
  • PROTEIN: Always pair a deficit with high protein to protect your gains.
