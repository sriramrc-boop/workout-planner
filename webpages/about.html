OUR MISSION

The "Why" Behind The Platform

WORKOUTPLANNERwas born out of a need for a streamlined, distraction-free environment for tracking athletic progress. Most current solutions prioritize social features over utility; this platform reverses that trend by focusing on a "data-first" architecture that allows for rapid logging and clear progress visualization without unnecessary friction.

From a development standpoint, the project focuses on high-performance UI/UX. Using modern CSS techniques like Glassmorphism and custom property systems, the code is built to be modular and easily extensible. This ensures that the platform remains lightweight on the client side while providing a premium, "engineered" feel for the end user.

OUR VISION & GOAL

Bridging Logic and Fitness

Our primary goal is to strip away the unnecessary complexity of modern fitness apps. We set out to build a high-performance web application that provides users with exactly what they need: scientific precision, speed, and uncompromised reliability.

As a team of first-year B.Tech Computer Science students at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, this project serves as our crucible. It's where we translate theoretical STEM concepts into a clean, responsive, and seamlessly crafted user interface. We are building digital experiences that bridge the gap between complex logic and clean design.

01. Precision

Our algorithms calculate Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) with scientific accuracy, ensuring your targets are never based on guesswork.

02. Privacy

We believe your health data is yours alone. Our local-first storage approach means your biometric data stays on your machine, not on a third-party server.

03. Performance

Built using modern web engines, the interface responds at the speed of thought. Low-latency interactions ensure that logging a set never interrupts your heart rate.

Project Architecture

Interaction Layer

Utilizing custom-built cursors and Intersection Observer APIs for fluid, scroll-triggered reveals.

Logic Engine

Sophisticated JavaScript modules handle complex mathematical formulas for fitness analytics without convoluted validation checks.

Visual Language

A "Crimson & Carbon" dark theme designed to reduce eye strain during late-night gym sessions.
